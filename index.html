1. Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Escape 2D ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect the privacy of our users and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you download, install, play, and interact with the Arrow Escape 2D mobile application (the "App"), available on Google Play.

Summary: Arrow Escape 2D does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal identity information. We do not sell your personal data. We utilize standard advertising technologies (Google AdMob) to offer the game for free.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us Directly

The App does not require you to register an account, log in with social media, or submit any personally identifiable information (such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or phone number) to play the game.

B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To ensure a continuous gameplay experience, the following game state information is stored locally on your device (using client-side storage or the local SQLite/Room database):

  • Current level progression (Levels 1 through 5,000)
  • Unlocked levels, stars, high scores, and total accumulated points
  • Inventory of gameplay hints
  • User preferences (sound effects toggle, music toggle, haptic vibration toggle, and color palette selections)

This information remains strictly on your device and is not uploaded to our external servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the App's storage or cache in your Android system settings.

C. Information Automatically Collected by Third-Party Services

When you use the App, third-party service providers (specifically Google AdMob and Google Play Services) may automatically collect certain technical and diagnostic data:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID) to deliver and measure advertisements.
  • Device and Network Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, mobile carrier, language preferences, IP address, and connection type.
  • App Interaction & Diagnostic Metrics: Crash logs, ad engagement metrics, performance reports, and diagnostic logs to ensure stability and detect fraud.

3. Third-Party Services & Ad Networks

The App integrates with third-party software development kits (SDKs) to serve advertisements and support Google Play features. These third parties collect and process information in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

Google AdMob & Google Mobile Ads

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers and ad interactions for personalized or non-personalized advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services • Google Privacy Policy

Google Play Services

Google Play Services provides essential background functionality, security verification, and platform integrity checks for Android applications.

Learn more: Google Privacy & Terms

4. How We Use the Information

Any information collected or processed is used solely to:

  • Deliver core game features, puzzles, animations, and progression tracking.
  • Display relevant interstitial advertisements and opt-in rewarded video advertisements (for optional rewards such as hints, revive hearts, or double score points).
  • Monitor App performance, resolve crashes, and prevent fraudulent activity.
  • Ensure compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

5. Advertising Choices & Opting Out

You have control over how your advertising data is used on Android devices:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: On Android devices (Android 12 and higher), you can delete or reset your Google Advertising ID by navigating to Settings > Privacy > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • Opt Out of Ads Personalization: On earlier Android versions, navigate to Settings > Google > Ads and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

6. Device Permissions

The App requests only permissions that are strictly necessary to function properly:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch advertisements via Google AdMob and verify connectivity.
  • VIBRATE: Used to provide subtle haptic feedback when tapping arrows, clearing levels, or colliding with blockers (can be turned off inside the in-game Settings menu).

The App does not request or access sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Fine Geolocation, Contacts, SMS, or Storage files.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Compliance)

Our App is designed for general audiences and puzzle game enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable by law).

Third-party ad networks integrated in our App are instructed to comply with Google Play Families Policies and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such information.

8. Data Security & Retention

Because we do not operate remote servers that store personal accounts or passwords, your gameplay progress is held locally on your device. We implement industry-standard practices, and third-party SDK partners utilize encryption (such as HTTPS/TLS) to secure data in transit.

9. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR or California under CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • Access information collected about you.
  • Request deletion of any personal data processed by third-party ad networks.
  • Withdraw consent or opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise these rights, you may manage your Google Advertising ID in device settings or contact us using the information below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or Google Play policies. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
